Link count after removing one.txt: Both remaining names show a link count of 2.
Is data gone after deleting one.txt? No, the data stays on disk because the inode still has a link count of 2 (pointed to by two.txt and three.txt).
When would it be gone? The data is only deleted when all names pointing to its inode are removed (link count drops to 0) and no process has it open.
